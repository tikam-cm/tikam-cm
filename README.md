## 🎯 My Engineering Philosophy

I believe good software engineering is about making the right technical
decisions, not just implementing features.

I value:

- **Architecture:** Designing systems with clear boundaries and responsibilities.
- **Reliability:** Building applications that handle failures predictably.
- **Security:** Treating authentication, authorization, and data protection as core requirements.
- **Maintainability:** Writing software that teams can understand and evolve.
- **Scalability:** Making informed trade-offs based on real requirements.
- **Business impact:** Solving the underlying problem rather than adding unnecessary complexity.

My goal is to grow from delivering individual software components to designing
complete systems that balance business requirements, engineering quality,
security, performance, and operational cost.
